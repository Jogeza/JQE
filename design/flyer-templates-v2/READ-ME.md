# JQE flyer templates — updated campaign pack

Website: **https://jqe.jokiholdings.com**

Telegram: **https://t.me/jqetrading** / **@jqetrading**

Four creative directions are included, each as a 1080 × 1350 social SVG and a 210 × 297 mm A4 SVG:

1. `01-editorial`: forest green, bold headline and oversized Q motif.
2. `02-research`: warm ivory, an editorial feature list and a geometric focus motif.
3. `03-campaign`: lime, strong typography and three graphic panels.
4. `04-photo-led`: a dedicated replaceable photograph panel. Replace the panel before publishing this design.

## Edit in Illustrator

Open the SVG directly with **File → Open**, then save an **Adobe Illustrator (.ai)** master. All copy is live text using Arial / Arial Bold. Named groups separate the logos, headlines, graphic artwork, contact block and QR codes. Illustrator may place these groups within a single layer.

The original Weltrade logo remains vector artwork. The JQE lockup is an editable reconstruction of the website's pulse symbol and wordmark with its Quant descriptor, using Arial in place of the site's Poppins font.

## QR codes

Every flyer contains two real, separate QR codes with a white quiet zone:

- WEBSITE → https://jqe.jokiholdings.com
- TELEGRAM → https://t.me/jqetrading

Standalone SVG and PNG codes are also provided for other materials. Keep the white border, square proportions and high contrast. Avoid adding a logo over the modules. At the supplied A4 scale each QR square is approximately 26.4 mm wide.

All 16 embedded codes were decoded from rendered artwork and matched their expected URLs. See `QR-VALIDATION.txt`. This validates encoding, not destination availability. Before publication, scan the final printed/exported artwork on a phone and check the destination after any edits or rescaling.

If a URL changes, generate a new QR code; editing the printed URL will not change its QR destination.

## Images and export

Read `IMAGE-RECOMMENDATIONS.md` for the image brief, crop sizes and Illustrator clipping-mask instructions. No stock or AI photograph is included. Three designs are usable with their original vector illustrations; the fourth offers a photograph provision.

Social: export PNG using the artboard. Print: supplied A4 files are RGB with no bleed. Convert a copy using your printer's CMYK profile and add its requested bleed before exporting a print PDF.
