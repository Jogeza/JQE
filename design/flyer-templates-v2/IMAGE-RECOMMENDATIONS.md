# Image direction for JQE campaigns

Use real photographs and real JQE screenshots. The strongest image for this brand is the actual product in a believable working environment. The supplied geometric designs are complete without photography; the fourth template includes a dedicated image provision.

## Photo-led image specification

- Social panel: x=64, y=188, width=952, height=474 in a 1080 × 1350 artboard. Approximate crop: 2:1.
- A4 panel: x=64, y=188, width=952, height≈563 in a 1080 × 1527.43 viewBox. Approximate crop: 1.69:1.
- A photograph at least 2400 pixels wide is suitable for the A4 image area at approximately 300 ppi; retain the original until export.
- Use the same photograph with a separate crop for each size. Keep the full screen inside the central safe area.
- The existing panel is an intentional placement guide. Replace it before publishing that fourth design. The other three flyers are ready to use without a photograph.

## Place an image in Illustrator

1. Open the SVG and save an AI master.
2. Find the `03_Replaceable_Photo_Panel` group. Remove its placeholder graphics and labels.
3. Draw a rectangle at the panel coordinates above, then use **File → Place** for your photograph.
4. Scale and crop the photograph behind the rectangle. Select both and use **Object → Clipping Mask → Make**.
5. Embed the photograph when handing the file to another designer or printer, or package it with the AI file.

## Shoot brief

Shoot near a window with the monitor brightness reduced enough to preserve screen detail. Use a clean desktop, a neutral notebook and one pen. Take a wide horizontal frame and a tighter detail frame. Remove account numbers, balances, private messages and credentials from the screen before shooting. Use the real dashboard; do not fabricate performance, profits or trades.

Avoid cash, luxury cars, staged celebrations, generic glowing trading charts and generated people. They weaken the product story. If commissioning or licensing photography, ask for the workstation composition above and confirm promotional usage rights for the chosen image.

The pictured photo provision is a vector placeholder, not a supplied photograph.
