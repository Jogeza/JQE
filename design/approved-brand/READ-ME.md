## Edit in Illustrator

1. Open the SVG with **File → Open** and save an **AI master**.
2. Edit copy with the Type tool. All promotional text is live Arial / Arial Bold.
3. The groups identify the background photograph, shading overlays, headlines, contact details, vector logos and QR codes. The photograph is embedded, so it will not become a missing linked image when you move the SVG.
4. Replace the photograph by placing your chosen image behind the overlays and text. Use an artboard-sized clipping mask. Remove the old image after placement.
5. Keep headlines within their allocated space; shorten copy or reduce type size if needed. Keep the contact block and QR labels visible.
6. Export using the artboard. For print, ask your printer for the required CMYK profile and bleed. These sources are RGB and have no bleed.

## Approved visual rules

- Forest green base: #17352e; deep photographic shadow: #081d17.
- Ivory text: #f6f4ea; lime accent: #d3e8a0; secondary text: #b5c5b7.
- Full-background photographs with restrained green shading behind text.
- Bold, short headlines; generous spacing; one clear campaign purpose.
- JQE and Weltrade logo lockup at the top. Weltrade is the original site vector. JQE is the previously approved editable reconstruction, using Arial in place of the site's Poppins.
- Avoid decorative clutter, fabricated profits or promises of trading results.
