## Cost qualification and baseline status

Read-only history inspection returned **18 deal observations across four symbols**,
not 18 independent completed trades. Observed commission/fee fields were zero;
one FX Vol 20 observation had a -0.13 USD swap. Six symbols had no matching deals.
The [commission observations](2026-10-08-v2-campaign-evidence/commission-observations.json)
are evidence about those records, not a verified zero-fee tariff.

Weltrade's current [account-type overview](https://help.weltrade.com/en/articles/13434918-trading-account-types-overview-cent-micro-pro-syntx-and-raw-spread)
lists SyntX commission **from USD 3 per lot**. It does not specify the exact
applicable per-symbol/per-side demo schedule. That conflicts with assuming a
universal zero cost from this small demo history. Preserve the uncertainty;
do not silently use either zero or USD 3 as a verified account-specific fee.

The canonical backtest currently sizes simulation quantities, applies generic
spread/fee assumptions, anchors its own stop/target distances and reports realized
balance drawdown. Captured broker terms and spread sidecars do not automatically
make those results verified Weltrade USD or lots. An offline economics/cost model
must first be integrated and tested in that canonical runner; no terminal calls
belong inside replay. Account-currency sizing, lot-step/minimum feasibility,
commission timing, swap rollover and stop/fill semantics must be explicit.
