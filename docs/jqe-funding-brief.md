# JQE demo research funding brief

JQE is building an auditable research and controlled demo-execution workspace
for native Weltrade synthetic indices. It combines live terminal market data,
the canonical strategy/risk pipeline, durable submission safeguards, Telegram
chart alerts, and a decision journal explaining both trades and no-trade outcomes.

We are seeking support for forward research and infrastructure: reliable Windows
workstation operation, immutable market datasets, cost-aware out-of-sample
evaluation, an authenticated read-only hosted monitoring connection, and clear
evidence reports. Funding would accelerate measurement and verification.

Current evidence: the demo account and terminal identity are verified; guarded
forward evaluation is running; real market chart delivery to Telegram has been
accepted; analysis and decision reasons are recorded in UTC. No new demo fill was
needed or forced for the initial checks. Existing research has not demonstrated
positive expectancy. JQE makes no profitability or return guarantee, and real
account execution remains unsupported.

Research milestones are a frozen candidate specification, sufficient forward
samples, complete fill/close reconciliation, realistic spread/commission/slippage,
drawdown and net expectancy reports, uncertainty intervals, and held-out validation.
Negative results will remain part of the evidence.

Before publishing a funding campaign, the owner still needs to select the funding
target, budget, donation destination and campaign audience. Donation collection,
commercial pricing and payments are currently inactive. This is a draft for owner
review, not a published solicitation or promise of investment returns.
